# Instadapp

> Instadapp is a DeFi middleware company that scales decentralized finance for users and developers through Smart Accounts. Its core DSA (DeFi Smart Accounts / DeFi Smart Layer) protocol aggregates lending, DEX, and yield protocols behind a single upgradable, non-custodial smart-contract layer, driven by a composable `cast()` function. Additional products include Fluid (a capital-efficient lending/DEX protocol), Avocado (a smart-contract wallet with account, gas, and smart-contract execution), and Instadapp Lite (streamlined vaults). The developer surface is on-chain smart contracts plus first-party JavaScript/TypeScript SDKs — there is no first-party REST API.

## Products
- [DSA — DeFi Smart Accounts](https://docs.instadapp.io/): Upgradable non-custodial smart-contract accounts that cast composable DeFi transactions across many protocols.
- [Fluid](https://fluid.io/): Capital-efficient lending and DEX protocol.
- [Avocado](https://avocado.instadapp.io/): Smart-contract wallet with account abstraction, gas abstraction, and smart-contract execution.
- [Instadapp Lite](https://lite.instadapp.io/): Simplified DeFi vaults.
